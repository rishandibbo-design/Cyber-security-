MOBILE SCREEN SHARING

Share a phone screen with another phone.

Android and iPhone screen sharing requires a native app. This option opens Jitsi Meet (an external service) instead of ClearShare's desktop WebRTC viewer.

Step 1 — Get the Jitsi Meet app

Install Jitsi Meet on both phones. Screen sharing from mobile Chrome alone is not supported.

Download Jitsi Meet

Step 2 — Create a meeting invitation

Generate a random meeting link. This creates the link only; the meeting begins when you open it in Jitsi. The first person may need to sign in as moderator.

Keep this link private. People with the link may be able to join your meeting. Jitsi Meet is a third-party service, not this website's video server.
Open meeting in Jitsi

Step 3 — Share and watch

  1. On Phone A, open the generated meeting in the Jitsi Meet app and join it. Sign in if Jitsi asks the host to authenticate.
  2. On Phone B, open the same meeting link in the Jitsi Meet app and join.
  3. On Phone A, use Jitsi's Share screen option and approve the operating-system screen-sharing prompt.
  4. Phone B can now watch in Jitsi. Phone A can stop sharing at any time from Jitsi or the system sharing control.

Privacy: This feature does not secretly collect or monitor screens. The phone owner chooses when to share, and screen sharing remains visible. Calls happen on Jitsi's service, so review its privacy practices before sharing sensitive material.
